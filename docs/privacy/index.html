GameRoute privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

GameRoute is a navigation app for Android phones and Android Auto, made by Vyto-Line. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run servers that collect your data. To show maps, find places and plan routes, the app has to ask outside services, and those requests are listed below.

Your location

GameRoute uses your phone's location to show where you are, guide you, and warn you about cameras and speed limits. While you navigate it keeps using location in the background through a visible notification, so directions continue when the screen is off or you use Android Auto. Your location stays on your phone, except for the requests listed below.

Like any website, these services see your internet address when the app connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply to what they log.

Convoy and live arrival sharing (only if you turn them on)

When you start a convoy or share your arrival time, the app sends your position, speed, arrival time, destination and the name you chose to the people you invited. These updates pass through the public ntfy.sh relay, which keeps them for up to 12 hours. They are end-to-end encrypted: the key is in the code or link you share, so only people with that code or link can read them (not ntfy.sh, not us). Anyone you give the code or link to can see your position until you leave the convoy or stop sharing. Sharing stops by itself when you arrive.

Road reports (only if you send one)

If you report something on the road, such as an accident or a hazard, the type of report, its location, your direction and the time are shared with other GameRoute users so they can be warned. Reports don't include your name or any account, and other drivers see them only for a few hours.

What stays on your phone

Settings, saved places (Home, Work and favourites), trip history, offline map areas, cameras you marked and your convoy name are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Children

GameRoute is a driving app and isn't directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/Vyto-Line/GameRoute/issues.
